#Attributes</h2>
Here is a list of all attributes in the system. There are 3 possible actions on attributes:

 * Act (also known as execute or x)</li>
 * Get (also known as read or r)</li>
 * Set (also known as write or w)</li>

Many attributes support only one or two of the above actions. For example you can read the RSP attribute but you cannot set it.
